\problemname{Reserve Pathways}
A company has been contracted to create a connected set of pathways for a nature's reserve between major points of interest. They are quite greedy, so they want to add the cheapest sets of roads possible to ensure that all points of interest remain connected. The company has specifically entrusted you with the task of saving as much money as possible; they have provided you with a plan proposed by a \textit{previous} landscape architect, depecting a connected network. They would like you to report the total cost required to establish a minimised version of the provided network.

\section*{Input}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The first line contains an integer $1 \leq N \leq 10^5$, the number of points of interest, and $1 \leq E \leq N^2$, the total number of edges.
    \item Each other line contains 3 integers $u, v, w$, where $u$ and $v$ are points of interest, and $w$ is the price required to establish a path from $u$ to $v$.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Output}
Output a single integer: the total cost, which is the sum of the weights of the edges included in the network.
